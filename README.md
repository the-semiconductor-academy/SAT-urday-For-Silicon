# SAT-urday-For-Silicon

Technology is no longer built in isolated domains.

AI depends on hardware.
Modern software depends on computer architecture.
Robotics depends on embedded systems.
And intelligent computing depends on semiconductors.

But most students still learn these subjects separately, without understanding how they connect together in the real world.

That’s why we’re starting SAT (Saturdays) for Silicon → a new weekly initiative by The Semiconductor Academy.

To connect the fabric of modern systems and help students see the bigger picture behind today’s technology, we’ve designed these sessions to be deeply interdisciplinary in nature.

Every Saturday, we’ll explore topics across:
* AI systems
* Computer architecture and RISC-V
* Hardware & processors
* Embedded systems
* GPUs & accelerators
* Emerging technologies
* And the engineering behind modern computing

The goal is simple:
To help students understand how real-world technology works beneath the surface.

Not just theory.
Not just isolated subjects.
But the complete picture.

If you’re curious about the future of computing, this series is for you.
